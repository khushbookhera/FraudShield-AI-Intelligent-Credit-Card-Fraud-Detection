# How We Used IBM Bob

IBM Bob was used during the development of our Credit Card Fraud Detection
project as an AI-assisted development tool.

It helped us with different parts of the project, including understanding
the coding approach, improving Python code, finding and fixing errors, and
making the implementation easier to understand.

We also used IBM Bob to explore ideas and get suggestions while developing
the machine learning workflow.

The suggestions provided by IBM Bob were reviewed by our team, and we made
the final decisions about the code, approach, and implementation.

Overall, IBM Bob helped us work through development challenges more
efficiently and supported us while building and documenting the project.
